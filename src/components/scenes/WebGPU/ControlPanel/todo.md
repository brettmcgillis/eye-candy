## // Intent / Use Cases

Generative control panels — sliders, knobs, dials, jacks, switches, meters, screens and keys, laid out by recursive subdivision, a 4 → 2 → 1 block grid or circle packing (the Dial From Hell), so it is never clear whether the panel belongs to a vehicle, a lab instrument or a synth. After yasuo's "infinite widget" and 0b5vr's Inércia 2022 faceplate (`references/`), built as real geometry from one three-free kernel, with a ControlPanelCLI workbench for stills, videos and plotter SVG.
