# // Intent/Use Cases

- This scene will be used to generate many plots that follow a theme. The theme will be geometry arranged in a ring.

- The scene includes our typical preset controls for setting, resetting, and copying preset settings in the scene.

- The scene allows for selecting a background geometry so I can set the framing for the plot. The geometry could be a square, circle, or rectangular plane.
- The scene allows for setting the background geo size(s)
- The scene allows for setting the background geo rotation so a square could become a diamond.
- The scene allows for multple background planes & setting the spacing between planes. Ex, a triptych of rectangles

- The scene allows for selecting a main geometry from a drop down. square, circle. more in the future.
- the scene allows for tuning the size of the main geometry.
- the scene allows for tuning the rotation of the main geometry.
- the scene allows for adjusting the number of instances of the main geometry.
- the scene arranges the instanced geometry in a ring around the center of the scene.
- the scene arranges geometry with staggered z positions to try and prevent intersecting geo.
- the scene allows for adjusting the diameter of the ring
- the scene allows for adjusting the stepped rotation of geo instances in the ring.
