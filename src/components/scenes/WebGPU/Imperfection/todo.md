## // Intent / Use Cases

A faithful TSL port of the Imperfection Shadertoy (full, lite and wild
bundles): one hand-authored colour field, and every block, drip, hatch and
grain comes from distorting the lookup into it. Rolled variations (layout,
palette, paint, seed) for print batches; its paint rig is the paint pass under
Untitled1 (Gregory Lysak study).
