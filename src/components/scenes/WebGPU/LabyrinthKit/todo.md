# // Intent

Toolbox for the House of Leaves constellation. Each architectural piece —
stair flight, wall segment, landing, hallway, room, flare — isolated at a fixed
camera under neutral light so it can be designed, scaled and inspected on its
own, plus an assembled preview that checks the pieces actually meet.

Geometry lives in `@modules/houseOfLeaves` so scenes and this toolbox share it
without importing across scenes.
