## // Intent / Use Cases

- Realistic fine threads in macro: a bed of parallel colored threads with a twisted, frizzy bundle draped across it. Inspired by a Houdini fiber sim.
- Fiber motion is toggleable: animated noise (the sine-thread and looping-noise-ring mechanisms lifted into 3D) or a GPU verlet strand sim.
- The two reference Shadertoy shaders ship as faithful fullscreen presets.
