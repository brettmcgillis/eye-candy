## // Intent / Use Cases

Render and plot flocks of FakeBirds (the BirdsArentReal pigeon with its security-camera head) with the BirdsArentRealCLI — headless stills from seven views, idle / turntable videos and pen-plotter SVG/DXF of the posed meshes, rolling location, flock (cluster, line or ring), bird, pose and camera — or pose a running flock in the Live tab (gaze, stare at cursor, orbit), pause it and capture exactly what you see.
