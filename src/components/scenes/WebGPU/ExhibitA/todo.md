## // Intent / Use Cases

- One mathematical object on a plinth in Apollian's studio, read as a real
  museum piece: stills, and animations of it turning, being lit, evolving
  or being drawn in.
- Every exhibit is a solid you could hold — fractal fields, thickened
  surfaces, tubes, wire, strung thread. No point clouds.
- Families: escape-time fractals, algebraic surfaces, classical surfaces,
  strange attractors, knots & the Hopf fibration, string models, 4D
  polytopes, and Apollian's 4D Apollian / Pseudo-Kleinian as guests.
