## // Intent / Use Cases

Generative subdivided cubes after two 0b5vr path tracers (`references/`): the rect-subdivision cube with gold frames and the sparse glass octree, raster-rendered from one structure kernel, with a HyperCubesCLI workbench for stills, videos and plotter SVG.
