## // Intent / Use Cases

A short walkable descent: living room, the five and a half minute hallway, the
great room, the grand staircase, the shaft floor, and back to the living room.
Navigable by keyboard, pointer and touch, with an autopilot for recording the
endless stretches as infinite-scroll clips.
