# // Quinn's Playground

# // Intent / Use Cases

- The scene leverages the ecctrl module and seal character to provide a playable scene where the player can explore Quinn's art
- The scene provides custom materials to the ecctrlJoystick
- The scene will be built with the stock lowpoly seal but will later be updated with Quinn's seal (to be delivered)
- The scene includes Quinn's dice in one area
- The scene includes Quinn's boardgame (to be delivered) in an adjacent area.
- The scene includes images of Quinn's drawn art (to be delivered) in a gallery area where images are displayed in frames

- The scene can contain audio of Quinn's choosing. consider using howl. check the examples in dev/examples/demo-2023-rain-puddle, dev/examples/demo-2024-a-storm-at-midnight
