## // Intent / Use Cases

Render and plot the Dumpster Fire scene with the DumpsterFireCLI — headless stills from seven views, burn / turntable videos and pen-plotter SVG (the dumpster, litter and wall as slices, contours and creases; the fire and plume blobs; the smoke as stipple, flow lines and shading), rolling the litter, the wall's damage, the smoke system and its path, the lids, the light and the camera — or watch a generation burn in the Live tab, orbit it and capture exactly what you see.
