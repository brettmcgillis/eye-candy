## // Intent / Use Cases

Generate Brutalist structures in batches using the BrutalistCLI — headless stills on either stage (the forest at full scale, or the maquette on its plinth), road-drift and turntable videos, and pen-plotter SVG of the massing; hold facets (form, weather, site, mood) to roll variations of a structure you like, and save generations as presets in the scene for their stage.
