## // Intent / Use Cases

Weave generative Persian rugs in batches with the RugPullCLI — knot charts (cartoons), floor and wall renders, SVG cartoons; hold facets (design, border, mine, palette, age, room) to roll variations of a rug you like, and promote rugs to scene presets.
