## // Intent / Use Cases

A 3D version of zoso95's Hair turtle (turtletoy.net/turtle/36d793188c, CC
BY-NC-SA 4.0): every strand of the noise walk becomes a rubber cable coming
up out of one hole in the floor. Flat lays the walk on the floor, cables
piling into runs where the streamlines converge; Tangle steers the same walk
in 3D (a second noise sets pitch) so the cables erupt into a tangle above the
hole. Static for now; motion to come.
