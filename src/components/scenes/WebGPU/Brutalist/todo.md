## // Intent / Use Cases

Coming upon something enormous, old and unexplained in a forest: generative brutalist structures (monolith, habitable block, spomenik) at full 60–150m scale among still trees, weathered concrete and moss, under height fog that drifts — the only thing that moves. The plinth half of the pair is BrutalistMaquette; both draw from `@modules/brutalist`.
