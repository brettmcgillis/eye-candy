Experiments synchronizing scenes in separate tabs via localStorage. Presets include:

- **Cloud Connected** - My first experiment in spatial synchronization. Arrange the clouds on your desktop.
- **Waterworks** - PIC/FLIP fluid sim across tabs. Fluid particles are bound to the dimensions of their tabs and can only move freely between them when tabs are overlapping.
- **Radiance Cascades** - A radial shadow-map illuminated by each tab's light, and occluded by their assigned SDF. Rearranging tabs allows light to spill between and/or cast shadows where they overlap.
- **Gravity Rooms** - A 2D physics simulation where each tab controls the direction of the gravity within it's bounds via street-sign orientation. A bouncing ball is forced to obey these traffic laws and is limited to the bounds of the browser tabs and where they overlap.
