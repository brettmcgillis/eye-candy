## // Intent / Use Cases

A CRT television sits on the floor, watched by a ring of surveillance
cameras on stands. The screen plays whichever camera's feed is tuned in, so
the picture recurses into itself; changing channels changes camera. Cables
run from every camera and from the TV's front and back jacks, settled into a
tangle on the floor. The whole view is post-processed as a CRT monitor
watching another, unseen camera.
