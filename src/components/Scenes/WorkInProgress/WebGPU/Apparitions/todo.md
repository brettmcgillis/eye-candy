# // Intent / Use Cases

- build a scene fully cenetered around particles & mediapipe fullbody/face detection, to be projected on a wall as viewers walk past, with a webcam pointed at the viewers.
- scene contains colorful particles moving through space on a black background.
- the bounding box for the space the particles occupy is illustrated with white lines/cube wireframe.
- The scene uses the webcam to find n people and push their positions/landmark positions into the particle system to act as attractors/repellers. we have the fullbody stuff (BoneZone), we have particles & attractors/repellers (particle lab, etc).
- camera orbits the cube/space to appreciate the particles from all angles.
- as the viewer moves the particle storms loosely follow their position making the viewer apparate in the particles like smoke.
- when the scene is in attractor mode the particles form the viewer
- when the scene is in repeller mode the particles clear the way from the viewer.
- refer to `dev/examples/flow`. We want to first port this over and then build in interactivity
